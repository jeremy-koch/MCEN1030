Base Camp

Monday office hours

Mondays are an optional session/office hour. The first half of the semester, they are Base Camp: particularly useful if you are new to all of this, but open to everyone. No attendance is taken, nothing here affects your grade, but you will have a better grasp of the material at the end of each session. Plus, it’s a good way to meet some more people in the class and to talk to me/the TA!

In the second half of the semester, Mondays become Side Quests — interesting extensions beyond the core course content. Still optional.

The sessions

BC1 (Week 1) · The computer itself, and how to talk about code. Before any coding: the computer itself. Browsers vs. apps, Colab login, where a downloaded file goes, folders, uploading to Colab, what “the cloud” means for your work. Then the second half: how to ask a question about code — the vocabulary (what do you call **, [], :?), the best way to ask a question: what I ran / what I expected / what I got instead.
